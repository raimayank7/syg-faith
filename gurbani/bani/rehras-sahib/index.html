Rehras Sahib

The evening prayer of the Sikh Nitnem, recited at sunset.

About Rehras Sahib

Rehras Sahib is the Sikh evening prayer. It brings together hymns from the Guru Granth Sahib by several Gurus, beginning with "So Dar", followed by a portion from the Dasam Granth that includes Chaupai Sahib, and ends with a short form of Anand Sahib and closing verses of the Guru Granth Sahib.

The word rehras comes from rah, the path, and ras, the taste or essence: the prayer of the path. It is a bani of thanks for the day that has passed.

When it is recited

Rehras Sahib is recited in the evening, at or after sunset, and it is recited at gurdwaras every evening. A full reading takes about 15 to 25 minutes.

The database lists 530 lines in 182 parts, containing hymns from more than one source.

naanak naam milai taa(n) jeevaa(n) tan man theevai hariaa ||1||

ਓਥੈ ਤੇਰੀ ਰਖ ਅਗਨੀ ਉਦਰ ਮਾਹਿ ॥

ਸੁਣਿ ਕੈ ਜਮ ਕੇ ਦੂਤ ਨਾਇ ਤੇਰੈ ਛਡਿ ਜਾਹਿ ॥

ਭਉਜਲੁ ਬਿਖਮੁ ਅਸਗਾਹੁ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਪਾਰਿ ਪਾਹਿ ॥

bhaujal bikham asagaahu gur sabadhee paar paeh ||

ਜਿਨ ਕਉ ਲਗੀ ਪਿਆਸ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਸੇਇ ਖਾਹਿ ॥

jin kau lagee piaas a(n)mrit sei khaeh ||

ਕਲਿ ਮਹਿ ਏਹੋ ਪੁੰਨੁ ਗੁਣ ਗੋਵਿੰਦ ਗਾਹਿ ॥

kal meh eho pu(n)n gun govi(n)dh gaeh ||
