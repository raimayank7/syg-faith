Kirtan Sohila

The bani recited before sleeping, and at the funeral ceremony.

About Kirtan Sohila

Kirtan Sohila, "the song of praise", is a short bani in the Guru Granth Sahib. It has five shabads, by Guru Nanak Dev Ji, Guru Ram Das Ji and Guru Arjan Dev Ji. It is recited at night before going to sleep, as the closing prayer of the day, and it is also recited at the funeral ceremony, when it is read at the cremation.

It reminds the reader that the day is ending and that the time of leaving the world will also come, and it asks for the grace to be ready.

When it is recited

At night before sleep. It is also read at the Sikh funeral ceremony and at the closing of the Guru Granth Sahib for the night in the gurdwara.

Structure

Five shabads. The database lists 146 lines in 66 parts.
