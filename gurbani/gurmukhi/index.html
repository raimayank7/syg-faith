Learn the Gurmukhi script

The 35 letters and the vowel signs, with sounds in English letters.

Gurmukhi, "from the mouth of the Guru", is the script in which Gurbani is written. The traditional alphabet is the Paintee Akhri, a list of 35 letters taught in a fixed order. Gurmukhi is written left to right, like English. Every consonant carries an inherent short "a" sound unless a vowel sign changes it.

Vowels and signs

Three letters (ੳ, ਅ, ੲ) are the "bearers" of vowels. The vowel sounds are marked by signs placed around the letter, which is why Gurmukhi looks different from Devanagari.

Reading tips

  • In Gurbani the sign ੁ (aunkar) or ਿ (sihari) at the end of a word is often not pronounced; this is a rule of Gurbani grammar that a teacher will show you.
  • The sign ॥ marks the end of a stanza and ॥੧॥ a numbered one.
  • Practise with the opening line of Japji Sahib, the Mool Mantar, with the transliteration beside it.

This page gives general information in plain words. For how to practise, ask your granthi or a teacher at your gurdwara.
