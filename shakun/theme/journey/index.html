Omens for journeys and new beginnings

What families say before leaving, travelling or starting something

Many omens are about the start of something, such as a journey, an exam, a new job or a ceremony. They are often a way to calm nerves, which is part of their lasting use.

6 entries

Leaving with the right footMany families say to step out with the right foot first for a good start.
Seeing a full pot or a cow on leavingConsidered a good sign for a journey.
Being called back as you leaveIn some families, it is a reason to sit down for a moment before setting out again.
Pausing and drinking waterIf something goes wrong at the start of a journey, many people sit, drink water and begin again with a short prayer.
Taking sweets or curd before a journeyAn old custom to wish a good journey and a good return.
Starting on an auspicious dayMany people avoid beginning on days considered inauspicious in the panchang; see the Rahu Kaal section of this site for the traditional timings.

This page reports popular beliefs for interest. Omens do not predict anything, and nothing here is advice. Please do not cancel anything important, such as a medical appointment, an exam or a journey, because of an omen.
