Omens from the body

Eye twitching, itching palm, sneezing and more

Body omens are the most commonly mentioned in everyday talk. They are usually given in pairs for the right and the left side and for men and women, and the pairs differ between regions. A twitching eye is most often only a tired muscle; a doctor should be consulted if it goes on for weeks.

8 entries

Eye twitchingTradition says that the right eye twitching is a good sign for men and the left eye for women, and the opposite for the other eye, but there are many versions.
Itching palmIn India, an itch on the right palm is said to mean money is coming, and on the left that money will go out; in some places it is the reverse.
SneezingA single sneeze when starting something is said to be a reason for a short pause; two sneezes are said to be a good sign.
HiccupsPopularly said to mean that someone is remembering you.
Ringing in the earIn some places, the right ear ringing is said to mean that someone speaks well of you, and the left that someone speaks ill.
Itching footSaid in some places to mean a journey coming.
Tingling lipSaid in some places to mean food or a feast coming.
Yawning at the start of workA reason to take a deep breath and begin again, as some elders say.
