Milk boiling over: meaning

What it is said to mean in tradition, what is really happening and a sensible way to think about it.

In popular belief

Letting the milk boil over in a new home on the day of griha pravesh is a favoured custom in many parts of India, as it is thought to bring overflowing prosperity. On any other day, many cooks simply count it as a mistake with the stove.

What is really happening

Milk boils over because the protein film that forms on its surface traps steam. A deep pot, a lower flame and watching it help.

A sensible way to think about it

Enjoy the custom at griha pravesh. On other days, watch the pot.
