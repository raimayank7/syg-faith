Cat crossing your path: meaning

What it is said to mean in tradition, what is really happening and a sensible way to think about it.

In popular belief

In many parts of India a cat crossing your path from right to left is read as a sign of a delay or an obstacle. The usual response is to wait for a moment, for someone else to cross first, or to say a short prayer before going on. In other regions the belief is weaker or reversed.

What is really happening

A cat is a silent animal that moves fast and appears suddenly, which startles people, especially in the dark. The belief that it brings bad luck is found in many countries and probably started with that sudden start.

A sensible way to think about it

If you are anxious about starting a task, take a breath, wait a few seconds and begin. Do not cancel a real appointment because of a cat.

Questions people ask

What does cat crossing your path mean?

In many parts of India a cat crossing your path from right to left is read as a sign of a delay or an obstacle. The usual response is to wait for a moment, for someone else to cross first, or to say a short prayer before going on. In other regions the belief is weaker or reversed.
