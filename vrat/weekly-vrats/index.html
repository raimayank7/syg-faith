Weekly vrats

The weekday vrats of Hindu tradition: Monday for Shiva, Tuesday for Hanuman, Wednesday for Ganesha, Thursday for Vishnu, Friday for the goddess and Saturday for Shani, with the usual practice.

Many families keep a weekly vrat. The deity of the day differs by region and family; the table gives the most common choices.

DayDeityCommon practice
SundaySuryaOne meal without salt in some families; offering water to the sun
MondayShivaFast until evening, bilva leaves and water on the lingam; 16 Mondays (Solah Somvar) is a popular vow
TuesdayHanuman, MangalaOne meal, reading the Hanuman Chalisa
WednesdayGanesha, Vishnu in some regionsGreen food, Ganesha puja
ThursdayVishnu, Brihaspati, Sai BabaYellow clothes and food, banana worship in some places
FridayLakshmi, Durga, Santoshi MataSweets, no sour food in the Santoshi Mata vrat
SaturdayShani, HanumanOil lamp, feeding the poor

For the gods see our weekday guide.
