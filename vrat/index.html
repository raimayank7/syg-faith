Vrat and fasting guide

What a vrat is, the main fasts of the year, what to eat and how to fast safely.

Start with what a vrat is.

How to use this guide

A vrat is a personal and family practice, and families keep it in different ways. This guide describes what is common, and says so where practice differs. If you are new to fasting, read what a vrat is, then the types of fast, and only then pick one: a weekly vrat such as Monday or Thursday is the usual first choice, because it comes every week and you learn how your body responds. Fasting and health is worth reading before you start, especially if you have diabetes, blood pressure, a stomach condition or are pregnant; the tradition itself allows a lighter fast or a symbolic one when the body needs it.

The guides on Ekadashi, Pradosh and Sankashti and Navratri explain the fasts tied to the lunar calendar. The exact day and the time to break the fast depend on your city and on the panchang, so we do not print dates; ask your temple or priest, or use a panchang for your own place. For what to eat, see vrat food ideas.
