Questions people ask

What is the Dhammapada?

A collection of 423 short verses of the Buddha in 26 chapters, one of the best-known books of the Pali Canon.

Which translation is this?

F. Max Muller\'s of 1881, in the public domain.

The verses are F. Max Muller\'s English translation of 1881, in the public domain; its wording is of its time. The notes are our own. This site is an introduction and not a substitute for a teacher, and it is independent of any Buddhist organisation.
