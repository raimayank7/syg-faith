Packing list for Hajj and Umrah

A practical packing list for Hajj and Umrah: ihram clothing, unscented toiletries, footwear, documents, medicines, money, a small bag and what to leave at home.

  • Documents: passport, visa and permit as given by your authorised organiser, vaccination certificate, flight tickets, copies of all of these and passport photographs.
  • Ihram: two sets of ihram cloth for men, a belt, safety pins (check if allowed), and modest loose clothing for women, with enough underclothes.
  • Footwear: comfortable open sandals for ihram, and another pair for walking.
  • Toiletries: unscented soap, shampoo and deodorant; toothbrush; towel; nail clippers for use after ihram.
  • Medicines and first aid: regular medicines, pain reliever, oral rehydration salts, plasters, a thermometer, sun cream (unscented).
  • Small day bag, water bottle, umbrella, a prayer mat.
  • Money: a limited amount in cash and a card, in a pouch under the clothes.
  • Phone and power bank with a local SIM if allowed.

Leave valuables, jewellery and large luggage at home. Ask your group organiser for the baggage limits.
