How this guide is arranged

Hajj is the pilgrimage to Makkah that every Muslim who is able should make once in a lifetime, and Umrah is the shorter visit that can be made at any time of year. Both have a fixed order of rites, and many pilgrims go in a group with a guide, so the pages here are written to help you understand what you will be doing before you go, not to replace a trained guide or your own scholar. They are in plain English, with the Arabic terms explained as they appear.

Read what Hajj is first, then the pages on the rites in order, then the practical pages on health, packing and the glossary. Schools of thought differ on some details, and the rules, quotas, fees and procedures are set each year by the authorities in Saudi Arabia, so check the official sources and the instructions of your tour operator for the current position. We do not list costs or dates of application.
