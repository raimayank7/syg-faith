Chanakya Niti by topic

Verses gathered by subject.

Seven topics gather the verses for which we have written a gist.

Each topic page lists the verses on one subject: friendship and trust, learning, character and conduct, wealth, prudence and planning, family, and the choice of places and company. A verse is shown in Sanskrit with Roman letters, and a gist in plain English follows. The gists are short summaries in our own words, not translations, and a saying that looks simple in English often has more than one meaning in the original, so treat the gist as a way in.

The sayings come from an old society and reflect its views. Some are practical and still useful, such as being careful whom you trust or not putting all your effort in one place. Some, on women, birth and class, are not views we share, and we show them as they stand without comment. Pick the topic that matches the question you came with, read the gists first and then the Sanskrit, and if a verse interests you, open its chapter to read what comes before and after.

  • Friendship and trust
  • Learning and knowledge
  • Character and conduct
  • Wealth and fortune
  • Prudence and planning
  • Family and children
  • Choosing places and company

The Sanskrit is from a volunteer-made online edition and may contain slips; the gists are our own plain summaries, not a translation. Maxims reflect the society and the views of their time, and some verses about women, birth and class are not views we share; we show the text as it is and do not comment on those verses.
