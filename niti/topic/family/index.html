Family and children

Chanakya Niti on parents, children and the home

The verses on family describe what makes a good relationship (respect, care, trust and peace) and give a plan for children, from indulgence in early years to friendship at sixteen. The wording belongs to its time.

The verses

Chanakya Niti 2.4

ते पुत्रा ये पितुर्भक्ताः स पिता यस्तु पोषकः । तन्मित्रंयत्रविश्वासःसा भार्या यत्र निवृतिः

te putrā ye piturbhaktāḥ sa pitā yastu poṣakaḥ | tanmitraṃyatraviśvāsaḥsā bhāryā yatra nivṛtiḥ

In plain words: A son is one who respects his father; a father is one who looks after his children; a friend is one in whom you can trust; a spouse is one in whom you find peace.

Chanakya Niti 3.3

सत्कुले योजयेत्कन्यां पुत्रं विद्यासु योजतेत् । व्यसने योजयेच्छत्रुं मित्रं धर्मे नियोजयेत्

satkule yojayetkanyāṃ putraṃ vidyāsu yojatet | vyasane yojayecchatruṃ mitraṃ dharme niyojayet

In plain words: Settle a daughter in a good family, set a son to study, put an enemy in trouble and lead a friend to right conduct (dharma).

Chanakya Niti 3.16

एकेनापि सुपुत्रेण विद्यायुक्तेन साधुना । आल्हादितं कुलं सर्वं यथा चन्द्रेण शर्वरी

ekenāpi suputreṇa vidyāyuktena sādhunā | ālhāditaṃ kulaṃ sarvaṃ yathā candreṇa śarvarī

In plain words: One good, learned and virtuous child delights a whole family, as the moon lights the night.

Chanakya Niti 3.18

लालयेत्पञ्चवर्षाणि दश वर्षाणि ताडयेत । प्राप्ते तु षोडशे वर्षे पुत्रं मित्रवदाचरेत्

lālayetpañcavarṣāṇi daśa varṣāṇi tāḍayeta | prāpte tu ṣoḍaśe varṣe putraṃ mitravadācaret

In plain words: Indulge a child in the first five years, guide with discipline for the next ten, and from sixteen treat him as a friend. (The verse says "beat"; today we read it as firm guidance and do not endorse corporal punishment.)

The Sanskrit is from a volunteer-made online edition and may contain slips; the gists are our own plain summaries, not a translation. Maxims reflect the society and the views of their time, and some verses about women, birth and class are not views we share; we show the text as it is and do not comment on those verses.
