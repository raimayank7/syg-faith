Bible verses about peace

Peace of mind and peace between people

Peace in these verses means calm of mind as well as good relations among people. Some of them are spoken by Jesus to his friends before his death.

The verses

John 14:27

Peace I leave with you. My peace I give to you; not as the world gives, I give to you. Don’t let your heart be troubled, neither let it be fearful.

Philippians 4:6-7

In nothing be anxious, but in everything, by prayer and petition with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus.

Isaiah 26:3

You will keep whoever’s mind is steadfast in perfect peace, because he trusts in you.

Psalms 29:11

Yahweh will give strength to his people. Yahweh will bless his people with peace.

Romans 12:18

If it is possible, as much as it is up to you, be at peace with all men.

Matthew 5:9

Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called children of God.

John 16:33

I have told you these things, that in me you may have peace. In the world you have trouble; but cheer up! I have overcome the world.”

The Bible text is the World English Bible, a modern English translation in the public domain. Christians differ in how they read and apply the Bible, and the notes on this site are plain descriptions, not teaching. Ask your own church or teacher about what it means for you.
