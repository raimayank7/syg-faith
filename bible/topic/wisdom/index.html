Bible verses about wisdom

Wisdom and understanding

Wisdom in the Bible means skill for living, rooted in respect for God. The book of Proverbs is the main source, and James picks the theme up in the New Testament.

The verses

Proverbs 1:7

The fear of Yahweh is the beginning of knowledge, but the foolish despise wisdom and instruction.

James 1:5

But if any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of God, who gives to all liberally and without reproach, and it will be given to him.

Proverbs 2:6

For Yahweh gives wisdom. Out of his mouth comes knowledge and understanding.

Psalms 111:10

The fear of Yahweh is the beginning of wisdom. All those who do his work have a good understanding. His praise endures forever!

Proverbs 4:7

Wisdom is supreme. Get wisdom. Yes, though it costs all your possessions, get understanding.

Proverbs 9:10

The fear of Yahweh is the beginning of wisdom. The knowledge of the Holy One is understanding.

Where these passages come from

The 6 passages on this page are taken from 3 books: Proverbs, James, Psalms. Together they run to about 109 words, so the whole page can be read in 1 minute. A verse taken out of its chapter can lose some of its meaning, so each reference above links to the full chapter, and it is worth reading a few verses before and after the one you came for.

How to use these verses

People come to a topic page for many reasons: to prepare a talk, to find a verse for a card, or to read something quiet when they are worried. Read one passage slowly rather than all of them at once. Tap the verse number in a chapter to copy a single verse, and note the reference with it (for example, the book, chapter and verse and the letters WEB for the translation). If you plan to quote more than a few verses in print, check the terms of the translation: the World English Bible is public domain, but other translations are not.

The Bible text is the World English Bible, a modern English translation in the public domain. Christians differ in how they read and apply the Bible, and the notes on this site are plain descriptions, not teaching. Ask your own church or teacher about what it means for you.
