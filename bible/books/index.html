The 66 books of the Bible

Old Testament (39 books) and New Testament (27 books).

The Protestant Bible has 66 books: 39 in the Old Testament, the scriptures of ancient Israel, and 27 in the New Testament, which tells of Jesus and the early church. Catholic and Orthodox Bibles include a few more books (the Apocrypha or deuterocanonical books), which are not on this site.

In the table, the group tells you what kind of writing to expect: Law and History books are mostly stories, Wisdom books are poetry and sayings, the Prophets are warnings and hopes spoken to Israel, the Gospels tell the life of Jesus, and the Letters were written to early churches. The number of chapters helps you plan: a short letter such as Philemon is one chapter, a few minutes of reading, while Psalms has 150 chapters and Isaiah 66. BookGroupChaptersAbout
GenesisOT, Law50The beginning: creation, the first people, the flood, and the stories of Abraham, Isaac, Jacob and Joseph.
ExodusOT, Law40The rescue of the Israelites from slavery in Egypt under Moses, the Ten Commandments and the building of the tabernacle.
LeviticusOT, Law27Laws for worship, sacrifice, purity and holy living given to Israel at Mount Sinai.
NumbersOT, Law36The years of Israel in the wilderness, the censuses of the tribes, and the journey toward the promised land.
DeuteronomyOT, Law34Moses' final speeches before his death, restating the law and calling Israel to faithfulness to God.
JoshuaOT, History24Israel enters and settles the land of Canaan under Joshua.
JudgesOT, History21The time before kings, when leaders called judges, such as Deborah, Gideon and Samson, rescued Israel.
RuthOT, History4A short story of loyalty: Ruth, a woman from Moab, stays with her mother-in-law Naomi and becomes an ancestor of David.
1 SamuelOT, History31The prophet Samuel, the first king Saul, and the rise of the young David.
2 SamuelOT, History24The reign of King David, his victories, his great failure and the troubles in his family.
1 KingsOT, History22Solomon's reign and the temple, the division of the kingdom, and the prophet Elijah.
2 KingsOT, History25The later kings of Israel and Judah, the prophet Elisha, and the fall of both kingdoms.
1 ChroniclesOT, History29Genealogies from Adam and a retelling of David's reign with a focus on worship.
2 ChroniclesOT, History36Solomon, the temple, and the kings of Judah to the exile in Babylon.
EzraOT, History10The return of exiles from Babylon and the rebuilding of the temple and community.
NehemiahOT, History13Nehemiah rebuilds the walls of Jerusalem and renews the people's commitment to the law.
EstherOT, History10A Jewish queen of Persia, Esther, and her cousin Mordecai save their people from a plot.
JobOT, Wisdom42The suffering of a good man and his long conversation with friends and with God about why the innocent suffer.
PsalmsOT, Wisdom150150 songs and prayers of praise, lament, thanks and trust, many ascribed to David.
ProverbsOT, Wisdom31Short sayings on wisdom, work, speech, money and relationships, many ascribed to Solomon.
EcclesiastesOT, Wisdom12A reflective book that asks what lasting value there is in work, pleasure and wealth.
Song of SolomonOT, Wisdom8A poem of love between a man and a woman.
IsaiahOT, Prophets66Warning and comfort: judgement on Israel and the nations, and promises of a coming deliverer and a new age.
JeremiahOT, Prophets52The prophet who warned Judah before the Babylonian conquest and spoke of a new covenant.
LamentationsOT, Prophets5Poems of grief over the destruction of Jerusalem.
EzekielOT, Prophets48A prophet among the exiles in Babylon, with visions of God's glory and of the restoration of Israel.
DanielOT, Prophets12Daniel and his friends in Babylon, with stories of faithfulness and visions of the future of kingdoms.
HoseaOT, Prophets14God's faithful love for an unfaithful people, pictured through the prophet's own marriage.
JoelOT, Prophets3A plague of locusts becomes a call to repent and a promise that God will pour out his Spirit.
AmosOT, Prophets9A shepherd-prophet speaks against injustice and empty religion.
ObadiahOT, Prophets1The shortest book of the Old Testament: judgement on Edom.
JonahOT, Prophets4A reluctant prophet is sent to Nineveh, swallowed by a great fish, and learns about God's mercy.
MicahOT, Prophets7Judgement on corruption and a call to do justice, love mercy and walk humbly.
NahumOT, Prophets3The fall of Nineveh, the capital of Assyria.
HabakkukOT, Prophets3A prophet questions God about injustice and learns to trust.
ZephaniahOT, Prophets3The coming day of the Lord and a promise of restoration.
HaggaiOT, Prophets2A call to the returned exiles to rebuild the temple.
ZechariahOT, Prophets14Visions and promises to encourage the rebuilding of the temple and look to a coming king.
MalachiOT, Prophets4The last book of the Old Testament: a call to honest worship and faithfulness.
MatthewNT, Gospel28The life and teaching of Jesus, including the Sermon on the Mount, written with the Jewish scriptures in view.
MarkNT, Gospel16The shortest and fastest Gospel: the ministry, death and resurrection of Jesus.
LukeNT, Gospel24The life of Jesus with a focus on the poor, the outcast and on parables such as the Good Samaritan and the Prodigal Son.
JohnNT, Gospel21The life of Jesus told through signs and long conversations, with the statements beginning "I am".
ActsNT, History28The spread of the early church from Jerusalem across the Roman world, including the work of Peter and Paul.
RomansNT, Letter16Paul's long letter on sin, grace, faith and the Christian life.
1 CorinthiansNT, Letter16Paul answers problems in the church at Corinth, with the chapters on love (13) and the resurrection (15).
2 CorinthiansNT, Letter13Paul defends his ministry and writes about comfort in suffering and generous giving.
GalatiansNT, Letter6A letter about freedom: faith, not the law, as the basis of the Christian life.
EphesiansNT, Letter6God's plan for the church and how to live in unity.
PhilippiansNT, Letter4A warm letter of joy, written from prison.
ColossiansNT, Letter4The greatness of Christ and the new life of believers.
1 ThessaloniansNT, Letter5Encouragement to a young church and teaching about the return of Christ.
2 ThessaloniansNT, Letter3Further teaching about the return of Christ and steady work.
1 TimothyNT, Letter6Paul advises his younger colleague Timothy on leading the church.
2 TimothyNT, Letter4Paul's last letter, an appeal to Timothy to stay faithful.
TitusNT, Letter3Instructions on order and good conduct in the churches of Crete.
PhilemonNT, Letter1A short personal letter asking a slave-owner to receive back a runaway slave as a brother.
HebrewsNT, Letter13Jesus as greater than the prophets, angels and priests of the old covenant, with the chapter on faith (11).
JamesNT, Letter5Practical teaching: faith shown in deeds, control of speech, patience and care for the poor.
1 PeterNT, Letter5Encouragement to believers who suffer for their faith.
2 PeterNT, Letter3A warning against false teachers and a call to grow in knowledge.
1 JohnNT, Letter5On love, light and assurance of faith.
2 JohnNT, Letter1A brief letter on truth and love.
3 JohnNT, Letter1A brief letter of praise for hospitality.
JudeNT, Letter1A short warning to hold firmly to the faith.
RevelationNT, Prophecy22Visions of the final victory of God, written in symbolic language to churches under pressure.

The Bible text is the World English Bible, a modern English translation in the public domain. Christians differ in how they read and apply the Bible, and the notes on this site are plain descriptions, not teaching. Ask your own church or teacher about what it means for you.
