Isaiah in numbers

Isaiah has 66 chapters and 1292 verses, about 35,139 words in the World English Bible. Read aloud at an easy pace it takes roughly 4 hours 30 minutes. The longest chapter is chapter 37 (1051 words) and the shortest is chapter 12 (130 words).

How the book begins and ends

The first verse of Isaiah in the World English Bible reads: “The vision of Isaiah the son of Amoz, which he saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.” The last verse (chapter 66, verse 24) reads: ““They will go out, and look at the dead bodies of the men who have transgressed against me; for their worm will not die, nor will their fire be quenched, and they will be loathsome to all mankind.””

What kind of book this is

The prophets spoke to Israel and its neighbours in particular times of crisis. Their books are mostly poetry and often move quickly between warning, grief and hope, so a note about the time they were written helps.

If you want to read Isaiah, a good way is to read the one-line summary above, then read the chapters in order, a few at a time, and use the notes of your own church or teacher for what it means.
