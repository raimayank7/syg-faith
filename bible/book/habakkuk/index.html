Habakkuk

Old Testament: A prophet questions God about injustice and learns to trust.

About Habakkuk

A prophet questions God about injustice and learns to trust. It is a book of the Old Testament, in the group called Prophets. It has 3 chapters and 56 verses in the World English Bible.

Habakkuk in numbers

Habakkuk has 3 chapters and 56 verses, about 1,367 words in the World English Bible. Read aloud at an easy pace it takes roughly 11 minutes. The longest chapter is chapter 2 (537 words) and the shortest is chapter 1 (388 words).

How the book begins and ends

The first verse of Habakkuk in the World English Bible reads: “The revelation which Habakkuk the prophet saw.” The last verse (chapter 3, verse 19) reads: “Yahweh, the Lord, is my strength. He makes my feet like deer’s feet, and enables me to go in high places. For the music director, on my stringed instruments.”

What kind of book this is

The prophets spoke to Israel and its neighbours in particular times of crisis. Their books are mostly poetry and often move quickly between warning, grief and hope, so a note about the time they were written helps.

If you want to read Habakkuk, a good way is to read the one-line summary above, then read the chapters in order, a few at a time, and use the notes of your own church or teacher for what it means.
