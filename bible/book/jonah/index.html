About Jonah

A reluctant prophet is sent to Nineveh, swallowed by a great fish, and learns about God's mercy. It is a book of the Old Testament, in the group called Prophets. It has 4 chapters and 48 verses in the World English Bible.

Jonah in numbers

Jonah has 4 chapters and 48 verses, about 1,250 words in the World English Bible. Read aloud at an easy pace it takes roughly 10 minutes. The longest chapter is chapter 1 (490 words) and the shortest is chapter 2 (197 words).

How the book begins and ends

The first verse of Jonah in the World English Bible reads: “Now Yahweh’s word came to Jonah the son of Amittai, saying,” The last verse (chapter 4, verse 11) reads: “Shouldn’t I be concerned for Nineveh, that great city, in which are more than one hundred twenty thousand persons who can’t discern between their right hand and their left hand, and also many animals?””

What kind of book this is

The prophets spoke to Israel and its neighbours in particular times of crisis. Their books are mostly poetry and often move quickly between warning, grief and hope, so a note about the time they were written helps.

If you want to read Jonah, a good way is to read the one-line summary above, then read the chapters in order, a few at a time, and use the notes of your own church or teacher for what it means.
