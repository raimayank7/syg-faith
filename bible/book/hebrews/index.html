Hebrews in numbers

Hebrews has 13 chapters and 303 verses, about 6,873 words in the World English Bible. Read aloud at an easy pace it takes roughly 53 minutes. The longest chapter is chapter 11 (927 words) and the shortest is chapter 5 (309 words).

How the book begins and ends

The first verse of Hebrews in the World English Bible reads: “God, having in the past spoken to the fathers through the prophets at many times and in various ways,” The last verse (chapter 13, verse 25) reads: “Grace be with you all. Amen.”

What kind of book this is

The letters in the New Testament were written to churches or to individuals in the first century. Reading one is like reading one side of a conversation, so it helps to read the whole letter at one sitting, which usually takes only a few minutes.

If you want to read Hebrews, a good way is to read the one-line summary above, then read the chapters in order, a few at a time, and use the notes of your own church or teacher for what it means.
