Job 17

World English Bible, 16 verses. Tap a verse number to copy that verse.

« Chapter 16 · Job · Chapter 18 »

1“My spirit is consumed. My days are extinct and the grave is ready for me. 2Surely there are mockers with me. My eye dwells on their provocation. 3“Now give a pledge. Be collateral for me with yourself. Who is there who will strike hands with me? 4For you have hidden their heart from understanding, therefore you will not exalt them. 5He who denounces his friends for plunder, even the eyes of his children will fail. 6“But he has made me a byword of the people. They spit in my face. 7My eye also is dim by reason of sorrow. All my members are as a shadow. 8Upright men will be astonished at this. The innocent will stir himself up against the godless. 9Yet the righteous will hold to his way. He who has clean hands will grow stronger and stronger. 10But as for you all, come back. I will not find a wise man among you. 11My days are past. My plans are broken off, as are the thoughts of my heart. 12They change the night into day, saying ‘The light is near’ in the presence of darkness. 13If I look for Sheol as my house, if I have spread my couch in the darkness, 14if I have said to corruption, ‘You are my father,’ and to the worm, ‘My mother,’ and ‘My sister,’ 15where then is my hope? As for my hope, who will see it? 16Shall it go down with me to the gates of Sheol, or descend together into the dust?”

Job 17 at a glance

This is chapter 17 of 42 in Job, a book of the Old Testament (Wisdom). The chapter has 16 verses and 254 words in the World English Bible, so reading it aloud takes about 2 minutes. The chapter before it is chapter 16.

Short chapters are easy to read in one sitting; it helps to read the whole book through at least once to see where this one fits. The book is described in one line as: The suffering of a good man and his long conversation with friends and with God about why the innocent suffer.
