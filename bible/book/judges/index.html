About Judges

Judges in numbers

Judges has 21 chapters and 618 verses, about 17,749 words in the World English Bible. Read aloud at an easy pace it takes roughly 2 hours 17 minutes. The longest chapter is chapter 9 (1581 words) and the shortest is chapter 17 (376 words).

How the book begins and ends

The first verse of Judges in the World English Bible reads: “After the death of Joshua, the children of Israel asked of Yahweh, saying, “Who should go up for us first against the Canaanites, to fight against them?”” The last verse (chapter 21, verse 25) reads: “In those days there was no king in Israel. Everyone did that which was right in his own eyes.”

What kind of book this is

The history books follow the people of Israel in their land, through leaders, kings, wars and exile. They are written as narrative, so they read well from start to finish, but they often record events without comment, and the reader is left to judge.

If you want to read Judges, a good way is to read the one-line summary above, then read the chapters in order, a few at a time, and use the notes of your own church or teacher for what it means.
