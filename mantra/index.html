Questions people ask

What is the most powerful mantra?

Tradition does not rank mantras by power, and we cannot make such a claim. The Gayatri mantra is the most widely chanted.

Do I need a guru to chant these?

These widely known mantras are chanted by many families without initiation. For a mantra for your own practice, ask a teacher.

The meanings and notes are our own words and not an authorised translation. Mantras belong to living traditions, and families and teachers differ in how they chant and use them; please ask yours. We make no claim that any mantra brings any result.
