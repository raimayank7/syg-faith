What many faiths share

Most teach some form of the golden rule, care for the poor, honesty and restraint, and a time of prayer or reflection. Many also keep a day of rest or a day of fasting, and many hold festivals of light, harvest or new year around the same seasons.

Where they differ

They differ on the nature of God (one, many, or no creator), what happens after death (rebirth, heaven, or liberation), the role of scripture and of clergy, and the meaning of a good life. Mutual understanding begins by hearing each faith in its own words.

This is a short, neutral introduction written from widely agreed facts. Every faith has many traditions and its members may describe it differently; for a true account, ask a member of that faith. We state no figures and no dates of moveable festivals.
