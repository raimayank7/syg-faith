World religions explained

A short, neutral guide to twelve faiths: origin, texts, beliefs, practice, holy days and how to visit.

This guide introduces twelve of the world’s faiths in the same order and the same neutral tone, so that you can compare them fairly. For each faith you will find where it began, its main texts, what its followers believe and do, its holy days and how to behave if you visit a place of worship. It does not rank the faiths, and it does not argue that any is true or false; it describes what followers say about themselves. If you are a student, a teacher, a traveller or someone who has just met neighbours or colleagues of another faith, start with the page of that faith and the guide to visiting places of worship.

Each faith has one page of the same shape, so you can read two side by side. We do not give numbers of followers, because the figures differ between sources and change from year to year. If you spot anything that a follower of a faith would not recognise, please tell us through the contact page and we will correct it.

Questions people ask

Is this site about one religion?

No. It gives a neutral introduction to twelve faiths in the same order and tone.

Why are there no numbers of followers?

Figures change and differ by source, and a faith's worth does not depend on size.

This is a short, neutral introduction written from widely agreed facts. Every faith has many traditions and its members may describe it differently; for a true account, ask a member of that faith. We state no figures and no dates of moveable festivals.
