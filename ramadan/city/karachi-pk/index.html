Ramadan 2027 in Karachi

Ramadan 1448 is expected to begin on Monday 8 February 2027 in Karachi (UTC+5) and to last 29 days, ending with Eid al-Fitr expected on Tuesday 9 March 2027. In Karachi the fast lasts about 12 h 28 min on the first day and about 13 h 05 min on the last, because the days lengthen through the month. The times use the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi method (the usual choice for Pakistan) and local time (Asia/Karachi).
