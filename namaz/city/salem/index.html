Prayer times in Salem

Salem lies at 11.65°N, 78.16°E, time zone Asia/Kolkata (UTC+5:30). The table below uses the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi method, the usual choice for India, with Asr by the Hanafi rule. On 15 January Fajr is at 05:27 and Isha at 19:27; on 15 July Fajr is at 04:44 and Isha at 20:03. The gap between Maghrib and Isha is 75 minutes in January and 77 minutes in July.

Qibla from Salem

The Kaaba in Makkah is 4,220 km from Salem along the shortest path. Face west-north-west, a bearing of about 290 degrees measured clockwise from true north. A magnetic compass reads magnetic north, so correct it for the local declination; see how to find the Qibla.

Why is the mosque timetable different?

Mosques add a margin of caution and often list the iqamah (the time the congregation begins) rather than the start of the prayer time.
