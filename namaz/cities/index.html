The list holds the largest cities of India and the biggest cities of countries where many Muslims live or many Indians work: the Gulf, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Southeast Asia, North America, the United Kingdom and more. The column on the right is the Qibla bearing in degrees clockwise from true north. If your town is not here, use the nearest large city; within 50 km the difference is a minute or two.
