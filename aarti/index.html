Questions people ask

What is aarti?

Aarti is the ritual of waving a lit lamp before a deity, with singing, at the end of a puja and at fixed times of the day.

Which way do you wave the lamp?

Clockwise, in front of the deity.

Where are the words?

We do not print them; please use a printed aarti book from your family or temple.
