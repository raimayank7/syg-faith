What this site is

Every culture has superstitions: small beliefs about luck, numbers, animals and daily objects. This site collects the best-known from around the world, tells where they are thought to have started and says what is really going on. It is written for curiosity and for conversation, not for prediction. Superstitions do not affect what happens to you. Use the search box, or open a topic.

Questions people ask

What is the most common superstition?

Fear of the number 13 and the belief that a black cat or a broken mirror brings bad luck are among the best known in the West. In India the evil eye and the crow cawing are among the most widespread.

Are superstitions true?

There is no evidence that they change events. They persist because they are comforting and because people notice the confirming cases.
