Black cat: superstition explained

What people believe, where it comes from and what is really going on.

The belief

In the United States and much of Europe, a black cat crossing your path is said to be bad luck. In Britain, Ireland and Japan, the same animal is lucky, and a black cat is a gift to a bride.

Where it comes from

The link of black cats with bad luck is said to come from the Middle Ages, when cats were associated with witches. In ancient Egypt, by contrast, cats were sacred. In Japan, black cats were thought to bring good luck and keep away evil.

What is really going on

There is no connection between the colour of a cat and luck. Black cats are in practice often the last to be adopted at animal shelters, which is a good reason to treat the belief lightly.
