Spilled salt: superstition explained

What people believe, where it comes from and what is really going on.

The belief

Spilling salt is said to bring bad luck or a quarrel, and throwing a pinch over the left shoulder is said to blind the devil who waits there or to undo the harm.

Where it comes from

Salt was valuable and used in sacred rites, so spilling it was a waste and an ill omen. The scene in Leonardo da Vinci's painting of the Last Supper where Judas knocks over the salt is often cited, though it has no direct link to the origin.

What is really going on

If you spill salt, wipe it up, and throw a pinch over your shoulder if it makes you feel better.
