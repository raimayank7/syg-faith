The Good Samaritan

The Good Samaritan: a short retelling of the Bible story from Luke 10 for children, with questions, a memory verse and an activity.

Jesus told a story about a man who was attacked by robbers on the road and left half dead. A priest and a Levite passed by on the other side. Then a Samaritan, whom the man's people did not like, stopped, bandaged his wounds, took him to an inn and paid for his care. Jesus asked who was the neighbour, and said, "Go and do likewise".

Read it in the Bible

This story is in Luke chapter 10 of the Bible.

A verse to remember

Now which of these three do you think seemed to be a neighbor to him who fell among the robbers?”
Luke 10:36, World English Bible

Questions to talk about

  1. Who stopped to help?
  2. Why was it surprising that the Samaritan helped?
  3. Who is your neighbour?

An activity

Think of three ways you can help someone this week, and do one.
