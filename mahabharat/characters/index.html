Characters of the Mahabharata

Sixteen characters of the Mahabharata in plain words: Bhishma, Yudhishthira, Arjuna, Draupadi, Krishna, Karna, Duryodhana and others, with their role in the story.

The Mahabharata has hundreds of named characters, and tellings differ on many of them. These sixteen are the ones most often asked about: the five Pandavas and Draupadi, their cousins and their allies and enemies, and the teachers and elders who stand between the two sides. Each page tells who the character is, what they do in the story, and how they are seen today. A good order is to read the Pandavas first, then Karna, Duryodhana and Krishna, and last the elders Bhishma, Drona, Vidura and Dhritarashtra, whose choices shape the war before it begins.
