Bhagavad Gita 2.13

Chapter 2, verse 13: the Sanskrit, the Roman letters, the meaning and what it is used for.

2.13

देहिनोऽस्मिन्यथा देहे कौमारं यौवनं जरा
तथा देहान्तरप्राप्तिर्धीरस्तत्र न मुह्यति

dehino'sminyathā dehe kaumāraṃ yauvanaṃ jarā
tathā dehāntaraprāptirdhīrastatra na muhyati

Meaning

As the embodied one passes in this body through childhood, youth and old age, so it passes into another body. The steady person is not confused by this.

Explanation

Krishna uses a fact everyone has seen: the child you were and the person you are now share nothing in the body, and yet you feel you are the same "I". If that sameness can run through such different bodies, he argues, death is only one more change in a long series. This verse is the start of the teaching on the self and one of the most quoted on grief.

Where it fits
