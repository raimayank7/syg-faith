Questions people ask

How many verses are in the Bhagavad Gita?

Tradition counts 700 verses in 18 chapters. The common printed edition numbers 701, because chapter 13 starts with Arjuna's question as verse 1; this site follows that numbering.

Which verse of the Gita is the most famous?

Verse 2.47, "You have a right to action alone, never to its fruits", is the most quoted. Verses 2.20, 4.7 and 18.66 follow it.

Is the Gita part of the Mahabharata?

Yes. It is in the Bhishma Parva of the Mahabharata and contains the teaching Krishna gives Arjuna before the war.
