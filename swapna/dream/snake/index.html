Snake in a dream: meaning

What it is said to mean in tradition, what researchers say and a sensible way to think about it.

In popular belief

In popular Indian belief a snake in a dream is one of the most commented-on symbols. Because the snake is linked with Shiva (who wears one), with the nagas, and with the ancestors in some families, many read a calm snake as a blessing and an attacking snake as a fear of an enemy or of something hidden. A snake that bites can be read as a sudden worry; a snake that is killed as a problem being overcome. In some regions a family that dreams of snakes in a season is advised to offer milk at a snake shrine on Nag Panchami; this is custom, not rule.

What researchers say

Psychologists note that snakes are among the commonest fear objects in people all over the world, and that the dream usually appears in times of tension or when someone feels threatened without knowing where from. Dream researchers do not agree on any fixed meaning for any symbol.

A sensible way to think about it

It is fine to treat the dream as a thought about what you are afraid of. Ask yourself what in the last days felt like a hidden threat, and speak to someone about it. Do not make any decision about money, health or relationships because of a dream.
