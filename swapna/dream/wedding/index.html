Wedding in a dream: meaning

What it is said to mean in tradition, what researchers say and a sensible way to think about it.

In popular belief

A wedding in a dream is read as a new beginning or a change, not necessarily a marriage. A happy wedding is a good sign; a wedding where something goes wrong is read as worry about a decision.

What researchers say

The wedding is a symbol of joining and commitment in all cultures. The dream often appears when a person is making a big decision.

A sensible way to think about it

Take it as a prompt to think about what you are joining or committing to now.
