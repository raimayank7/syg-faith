Dreams about the body and health

Teeth, hair, blood, falling and flying

Dreams about the body are among the most commonly reported all over the world. In Indian belief they are mostly read as signs of change, worry and the state of the family. They are not a medical signal, and if you are worried about your health you should see a doctor, not a dream book.

12 entries

Teeth falling outCommonly read as a worry about family, money or ageing, and in some traditions as concern for a relative. Common in many countries and often linked with stress.
HairLong, healthy hair is a good sign; hair falling out is read as a worry about status or strength.
BloodA worry about the family or a conflict; in some readings the loss of an old burden.
FallingA fear of losing control or a feeling that something in life is not secure.
FlyingFreedom, ambition and joy; a favoured dream in many cultures.
Being naked in publicEmbarrassment or a fear of being judged; very common and almost always harmless.
Being sickA worry about health or a feeling of tiredness; in some beliefs a sign of recovery, not of illness.
HandsWork and action: clean hands a good sign, dirty or tied hands a worry about the work.
EyesUnderstanding and clarity; losing sight is read as confusion about a decision.
PregnancyA new beginning, a project or a wish for a child; often read as good news.
CryingOften read as a relief of feelings, and in some popular beliefs of happiness to follow.
LaughingOften read as the opposite warning: a worry that is being covered by a smile.
