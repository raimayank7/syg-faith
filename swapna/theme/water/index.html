Dreams about water and nature

Rivers, rain, the sea, fire, the moon and the sun

Water, fire and the sky have a long place in the Indian imagination, and dreams of nature are given many readings. The common idea is that clear and calm images are good signs and violent or dark ones are a sign of worry.

12 entries

WaterClear, calm water is read as peace of mind and health; muddy or rushing water as a worry or confusion in life.
RiverThe Ganga or any river is a favoured image, a symbol of purification and of moving on; a flooded river is read as a fear of being overwhelmed.
RainUsually good fortune and relief, as in the farming year; heavy storms are read as an anxious time.
SeaA large and calm sea is read as a long journey and a broad mind; a rough sea as strong feelings.
FireCan mean anger, passion or a sudden change; a lamp or the sacred fire is read as a sign of blessing and knowledge.
MoonCalm, beauty and a gentle mind; the full moon is a favoured sign.
SunSuccess, energy and honour; a rising sun is a hopeful sign.
StarsHope and wishes; seeing a fallen star is read as a wish ending or a change.
MountainA task or goal; climbing it is read as effort and success, and being stuck as a feeling of a heavy burden.
FlowersHappiness and good news; the lotus is linked with Lakshmi and purity.
TreeA strong tree is read as the family and its roots; a dry tree as concern for an elder.
GardenPeace, good company and slow progress.
