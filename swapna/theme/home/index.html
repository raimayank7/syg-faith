Dreams about the home and objects

House, door, mirror, clothes, food and vehicles

The house in dreams is usually the self or the family. Objects are given readings based on what they stand for in everyday life.

12 entries

HouseThe self, the family and security; a new or clean house is a good sign, a broken one a worry about the home.
DoorAn opportunity or a change; an open door is hopeful, a locked one a block.
MirrorHow you see yourself, or a wish for honesty; a broken mirror is read as a worry about one's image.
ClothesStatus and the face one shows to others; new clothes are a good sign.
FoodSatisfaction and a sense of enough; sweets are a favoured sign.
TrainA journey in life; missing a train is the most common worry dream about time.
Car or busDirection and control: driving well is a good sign, losing control a worry.
StairsProgress; going up is effort and success, going down a letting go.
Lamp or diyaKnowledge, hope and the blessing of Lakshmi; a steady flame is a favoured sign.
BookKnowledge and a decision to learn.
PhoneA wish for contact or a worry about a message; very common in modern dreams.
ExamA fear of being tested or judged. It is among the most commonly reported dreams, often long after school has ended.
