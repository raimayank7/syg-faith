Dreams about gods and sacred things

Gods, temples, prayer, the Ganga and holy places

Dreams of gods and temples are held in great respect and are often seen as a blessing (darshan). Families sometimes keep such a dream to themselves, or share it with an elder.

8 entries

TempleA good sign of peace and a wish for spiritual comfort.
God or goddessA blessing and a sign of protection, often taken as a call to prayer.
GangaPurification and a fresh start; a favoured dream.
PrayerA need for calm; and in popular belief a sign that a prayer is being heard.
Holy manGuidance; many read it as a sign to seek advice from an elder.
CremationA sign of ending: an old chapter closing, not of death itself, in the usual reading.
PilgrimageA wish for a journey of faith or for change.
Bell or conchA call to attention and, in popular belief, good tidings.
