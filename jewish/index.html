Questions people ask

What is today's Hebrew date?

Use the converter above: choose today\'s date. After sunset the Hebrew day is already the next one.

Why is Rosh Hashanah on a different date each year?

The Hebrew year is lunisolar, so a Hebrew date falls on a different Gregorian date each year.

Jewish holidays begin at sundown on the evening before the date shown. Dates follow the standard Hebrew calendar; communities outside Israel keep a second day of several festivals. Customs differ between Orthodox, Conservative, Reform and other communities, and this is not a ruling: ask your rabbi.
