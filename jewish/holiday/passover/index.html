Passover (Pesach)

The festival of freedom, 15 to 21 Nisan

Passover remembers the exodus of the Israelites from slavery in Egypt, on the fifteenth of Nisan, in March or April. It lasts seven days in Israel and eight in the diaspora. The home is cleaned of leavened food (chametz), matzah (unleavened bread) is eaten, and on the first night (and the second, outside Israel) the family holds the Seder, a meal that follows the Haggadah, the telling of the story, with four cups of wine, bitter herbs and symbolic foods on the Seder plate.

The greeting is "Chag Pesach sameach", a happy Passover.
