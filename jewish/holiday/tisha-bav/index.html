Tisha B'Av

The ninth of Av

Tisha B'Av is a day of mourning for the destruction of the First and Second Temples in Jerusalem and for other disasters in Jewish history. It is observed on the ninth of Av, in July or August, with a fast of about 25 hours, sitting low on the floor or on low stools, reading the Book of Lamentations and avoiding leather shoes and washing for pleasure. When the ninth falls on Shabbat, the fast is moved to the tenth.

It follows three weeks of mourning that begin with the fast of 17 Tammuz.
