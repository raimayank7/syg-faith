Questions people ask

What is the difference between Char Dham and Chota Char Dham?

The Char Dham are four shrines in the four directions of India: Badrinath, Dwarka, Puri and Rameswaram. The Chota Char Dham are four shrines of Uttarakhand in the Himalaya: Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath.

How many jyotirlingas are there?

Twelve, in the traditional list: Somnath, Mallikarjuna, Mahakaleshwar, Omkareshwar, Kedarnath, Bhimashankar, Kashi Vishwanath, Trimbakeshwar, Vaidyanath, Nageshwar, Rameshwaram and Grishneshwar.

Temple rules, opening seasons, booking arrangements and routes change. Before you travel, check the official website of the temple or the state tourism board, and be careful of anyone who asks for money to book darshan or a stay by phone or message.
