Indrajit (Meghanada)

Indrajit (Meghanada): who Indrajit is in the Ramayana and the role in the story, in plain words.

Ravana's son, the great warrior who defeated Indra, whose weapons laid Rama and Lakshmana low and who was killed by Lakshmana.

In the story

Indrajit is the eldest son of Ravana and Mandodari, also called Meghanada. He gained the name Indrajit, "conqueror of Indra", after defeating the king of the gods. In the war he twice binds Rama and Lakshmana with serpent weapons and almost wins it for his father. Lakshmana kills him, after a rite he was performing to become invincible is interrupted.

How he is seen

Indrajit is the great warrior of Lanka and a model of courage. He obeys his father out of duty, though some tellings show him as doubtful about the cause. His death is the turning point after which Ravana himself enters the battle.
