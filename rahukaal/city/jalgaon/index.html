Rahu Kaal in Jalgaon

Jalgaon lies at 21.00°N, 75.57°E in the time zone Asia/Kolkata (UTC+5:30). Rahu Kaal here is one eighth of the daylight. Over 2027 the daylight in Jalgaon runs from 10 h 51 min on 22 December to 13 h 24 min on 21 June, so Rahu Kaal itself lasts between 81 and 101 minutes. What is the Rahu Kaal in Jalgaon on Monday?

Why does Rahu Kaal in Jalgaon differ from a printed table?

Printed tables assume a day from 6 am to 6 pm. The real day in Jalgaon is 10 h 52 min on 1 January, so each eighth is 82 minutes, not 90.

Should I follow Rahu Kaal?

It is a tradition, not a rule of science. Many families avoid starting a journey, a purchase or a ceremony in it; many do not. Follow your own custom.
