Rahu Kaal in Jajmau

Jajmau lies at 26.43°N, 80.41°E in the time zone Asia/Kolkata (UTC+5:30). Rahu Kaal here is one eighth of the daylight. Over 2027 the daylight in Jajmau runs from 10 h 28 min on 22 December to 13 h 47 min on 21 June, so Rahu Kaal itself lasts between 79 and 103 minutes. On a typical Monday in January, with sunrise at 06:58 and sunset at 17:40, Rahu Kaal is 08:18–09:38 (8:18 am to 9:38 am).

Gulika Kaal and Yamaganda on a Monday in January

On 18 January 2027 Gulika Kaal falls at 13:39–14:59 and Yamaganda at 10:58–12:19 in Jajmau. Abhijit muhurat, the window around midday, is 11:57–12:40. Gulika and Yamaganda belong mainly to the South Indian panchang; many North Indian panchangs list only Rahu Kaal and Choghadiya.

Summer and winter

Questions people ask

What is the Rahu Kaal in Jajmau on Monday?

On a Monday in January it is about 08:18–09:38 in Jajmau. Use the finder above for today's exact time; it changes by a few minutes through the year with the sunrise.

Why does Rahu Kaal in Jajmau differ from a printed table?

Printed tables assume a day from 6 am to 6 pm. The real day in Jajmau is 10 h 30 min on 1 January, so each eighth is 79 minutes, not 90.

Should I follow Rahu Kaal?

It is a tradition, not a rule of science. Many families avoid starting a journey, a purchase or a ceremony in it; many do not. Follow your own custom.
