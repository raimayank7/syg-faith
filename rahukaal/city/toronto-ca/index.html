Why does Rahu Kaal in Toronto differ from a printed table?

Printed tables assume a day from 6 am to 6 pm. The real day in Toronto is 9 h 00 min on 1 January, so each eighth is 68 minutes, not 90.

Should I follow Rahu Kaal?

It is a tradition, not a rule of science. Many families avoid starting a journey, a purchase or a ceremony in it; many do not. Follow your own custom.
