Rahu Kaal in Hubballi

Hubballi lies at 15.35°N, 75.13°E in the time zone Asia/Kolkata (UTC+5:30). Rahu Kaal here is one eighth of the daylight. Over 2027 the daylight in Hubballi runs from 11 h 13 min on 22 December to 13 h 02 min on 21 June, so Rahu Kaal itself lasts between 84 and 98 minutes. On a typical Monday in January, with sunrise at 07:00 and sunset at 18:20, Rahu Kaal is 08:25–09:50 (8:25 am to 9:50 am).

What is the Rahu Kaal in Hubballi on Monday?

Why does Rahu Kaal in Hubballi differ from a printed table?

Printed tables assume a day from 6 am to 6 pm. The real day in Hubballi is 11 h 14 min on 1 January, so each eighth is 84 minutes, not 90.

Should I follow Rahu Kaal?

It is a tradition, not a rule of science. Many families avoid starting a journey, a purchase or a ceremony in it; many do not. Follow your own custom.
