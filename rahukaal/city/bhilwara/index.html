Rahu Kaal in Bhilwara

Bhilwara lies at 25.35°N, 74.64°E in the time zone Asia/Kolkata (UTC+5:30). Rahu Kaal here is one eighth of the daylight. Over 2027 the daylight in Bhilwara runs from 10 h 33 min on 22 December to 13 h 43 min on 21 June, so Rahu Kaal itself lasts between 79 and 103 minutes. On a typical Monday in January, with sunrise at 07:19 and sunset at 18:05, Rahu Kaal is 08:39–10:00 (8:39 am to 10:00 am).

Gulika Kaal and Yamaganda on a Monday in January

Questions people ask

What is the Rahu Kaal in Bhilwara on Monday?

On a Monday in January it is about 08:39–10:00 in Bhilwara. Use the finder above for today's exact time; it changes by a few minutes through the year with the sunrise.

Why does Rahu Kaal in Bhilwara differ from a printed table?

Printed tables assume a day from 6 am to 6 pm. The real day in Bhilwara is 10 h 35 min on 1 January, so each eighth is 79 minutes, not 90.

Should I follow Rahu Kaal?

It is a tradition, not a rule of science. Many families avoid starting a journey, a purchase or a ceremony in it; many do not. Follow your own custom.
