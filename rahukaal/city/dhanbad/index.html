Rahu Kaal in Dhanbad

Dhanbad lies at 23.80°N, 86.43°E in the time zone Asia/Kolkata (UTC+5:30). Rahu Kaal here is one eighth of the daylight. Why does Rahu Kaal in Dhanbad differ from a printed table?

Should I follow Rahu Kaal?
