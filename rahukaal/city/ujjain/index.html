Rahu Kaal in Ujjain

Ujjain lies at 23.18°N, 75.78°E in the time zone Asia/Kolkata (UTC+5:30). Rahu Kaal here is one eighth of the daylight. Over 2027 the daylight in Ujjain runs from 10 h 42 min on 22 December to 13 h 33 min on 21 June, so Rahu Kaal itself lasts between 80 and 102 minutes. On a typical Monday in January, with sunrise at 07:10 and sunset at 18:04, Rahu Kaal is 08:32–09:54 (8:32 am to 9:54 am).

Gulika Kaal and Yamaganda on a Monday in January

On 18 January 2027 Gulika Kaal falls at 13:59–15:21 and Yamaganda at 11:15–12:37 in Ujjain. Abhijit muhurat, the window around midday, is 12:15–12:59. Gulika and Yamaganda belong mainly to the South Indian panchang; many North Indian panchangs list only Rahu Kaal and Choghadiya.

Summer and winter

On 21 June sunrise in Ujjain is at 05:42 and sunset at 19:16, so a Monday Rahu Kaal falls 07:23–09:05. On 21 December sunrise is at 07:03 and sunset at 17:46, and the Monday Rahu Kaal falls 08:24–09:44. The shift between the two is 60 minutes at the start of Rahu Kaal.

Questions people ask

What is the Rahu Kaal in Ujjain on Monday?

On a Monday in January it is about 08:32–09:54 in Ujjain. Use the finder above for today's exact time; it changes by a few minutes through the year with the sunrise.

Why does Rahu Kaal in Ujjain differ from a printed table?

Printed tables assume a day from 6 am to 6 pm. The real day in Ujjain is 10 h 44 min on 1 January, so each eighth is 81 minutes, not 90.

Should I follow Rahu Kaal?

It is a tradition, not a rule of science. Many families avoid starting a journey, a purchase or a ceremony in it; many do not. Follow your own custom.
