Rahu Kaal in Nashik

Nashik lies at 20.00°N, 73.79°E in the time zone Asia/Kolkata (UTC+5:30). Rahu Kaal here is one eighth of the daylight. Over 2027 the daylight in Nashik runs from 10 h 55 min on 22 December to 13 h 20 min on 21 June, so Rahu Kaal itself lasts between 82 and 100 minutes. Questions people ask

What is the Rahu Kaal in Nashik on Monday?

On a Monday in January it is about 08:36–09:59 in Nashik. Use the finder above for today's exact time; it changes by a few minutes through the year with the sunrise.

Why does Rahu Kaal in Nashik differ from a printed table?

Printed tables assume a day from 6 am to 6 pm. The real day in Nashik is 10 h 56 min on 1 January, so each eighth is 82 minutes, not 90.

Should I follow Rahu Kaal?

It is a tradition, not a rule of science. Many families avoid starting a journey, a purchase or a ceremony in it; many do not. Follow your own custom.
