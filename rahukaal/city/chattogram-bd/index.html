Rahu Kaal in Chattogram

Chattogram lies at 22.34°N, 91.83°E in the time zone Asia/Dhaka (UTC+6). Rahu Kaal here is one eighth of the daylight. Over 2027 the daylight in Chattogram runs from 10 h 46 min on 22 December to 13 h 30 min on 21 June, so Rahu Kaal itself lasts between 81 and 101 minutes. On a typical Monday in January, with sunrise at 06:35 and sunset at 17:31, Rahu Kaal is 07:57–09:19 (7:57 am to 9:19 am).

Gulika Kaal and Yamaganda on a Monday in January

On 18 January 2027 Gulika Kaal falls at 13:25–14:47 and Yamaganda at 10:41–12:03 in Chattogram. Abhijit muhurat, the window around midday, is 11:41–12:25. Gulika and Yamaganda belong mainly to the South Indian panchang; many North Indian panchangs list only Rahu Kaal and Choghadiya.

Summer and winter

On 21 June sunrise in Chattogram is at 05:09 and sunset at 18:39, so a Monday Rahu Kaal falls 06:51–08:32. On 21 December sunrise is at 06:27 and sunset at 17:14, and the Monday Rahu Kaal falls 07:48–09:09. The shift between the two is 58 minutes at the start of Rahu Kaal.

Compared with Dhaka

The nearest other city in our list is Dhaka, about 211 km away. On 18 January 2027 (a Monday) its Rahu Kaal starts at 08:04, which is 8 minutes later than in Chattogram. A difference of this size is normal between towns that far apart: the sun reaches each place at a slightly different moment.

Questions people ask

What is the Rahu Kaal in Chattogram on Monday?

On a Monday in January it is about 07:57–09:19 in Chattogram. Use the finder above for today's exact time; it changes by a few minutes through the year with the sunrise.

Why does Rahu Kaal in Chattogram differ from a printed table?

Printed tables assume a day from 6 am to 6 pm. The real day in Chattogram is 10 h 47 min on 1 January, so each eighth is 81 minutes, not 90.

Should I follow Rahu Kaal?

It is a tradition, not a rule of science. Many families avoid starting a journey, a purchase or a ceremony in it; many do not. Follow your own custom.
