Rahu Kaal in Ulu Bedok

Ulu Bedok lies at 1.33°N, 103.93°E in the time zone Asia/Singapore (UTC+8). Rahu Kaal here is one eighth of the daylight. Over 2027 the daylight in Ulu Bedok runs from 12 h 02 min on 22 December to 12 h 12 min on 21 June, so Rahu Kaal itself lasts between 90 and 92 minutes. On a typical Monday in January, with sunrise at 07:13 and sunset at 19:16, Rahu Kaal is 08:43–10:14 (8:43 am to 10:14 am).

Why does Rahu Kaal in Ulu Bedok differ from a printed table?

Printed tables assume a day from 6 am to 6 pm. The real day in Ulu Bedok is 12 h 02 min on 1 January, so each eighth is 90 minutes, not 90.

Should I follow Rahu Kaal?

It is a tradition, not a rule of science. Many families avoid starting a journey, a purchase or a ceremony in it; many do not. Follow your own custom.
