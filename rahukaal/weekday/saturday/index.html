Rahu Kaal on Saturday

Which part of the day it is, and the time in 30 large cities through the year.

Saturday belongs to Saturn (Shani). Rahu Kaal is the third part of the day, mid-morning.

The rule is the same in every place: cut the time from sunrise to sunset into eight equal parts and take part number 3. On a day that runs exactly from 6 am to 6 pm each part is 90 minutes, so the textbook answer for Saturday is 9:00 am to 10:30 am. Real days are never exactly 12 hours, and sunrise in Delhi is not sunrise in Chennai, so the table below gives the times in the 30 largest Indian cities for the middle of January, April, July and October 2027.
