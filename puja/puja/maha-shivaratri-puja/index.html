Maha Shivaratri Puja (महाशिवरात्रि पूजा)

Maha Shivaratri is the great night of Shiva. Devotees fast, stay awake through the night and bathe the lingam.. Items needed and the steps, in plain words.

Who is worshipped and when

The puja is for Shiva. It is done: The night of Maha Shivaratri (in February or March).

Items you need (samagri)

Tick the items as you collect them. The first few are specific to this puja; the rest are the basic items for any puja. You can print this page as a checklist.

Steps

  1. Fast during the day as you are able, with fruit and milk if you need them.
  2. Bathe the lingam with water, then milk, curd, honey, ghee and sugar in turn, and then with clean water again (abhishek).
  3. Offer the bilva leaves, flowers and ash while chanting "Om namah Shivaya".
  4. The night is divided into four watches (prahar), and many do a short puja in each.
  5. Stay awake with prayer, songs or reading; break the fast the next morning with prasad.

Notes

Anyone who cannot fast or stay awake can do a short puja in the evening. The tradition is flexible, and what matters is devotion.

Questions people ask

When is Maha Shivaratri Puja done?

The night of Maha Shivaratri (in February or March). Check a panchang or ask a priest for the exact time in the current year.

Can it be done at home?

Yes. This outline is for a simple home puja. For a long ceremony, a priest is usually asked to lead it.

This page describes common practice in plain words. Families, regions and priests differ in the details, and your own family tradition or your priest comes first. Dates and muhurat (auspicious times) change every year: ask a priest or use a trusted panchang. Nothing here promises any result.
