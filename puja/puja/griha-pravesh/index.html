Griha Pravesh Puja (House-Warming) (गृह प्रवेश पूजा)

Griha pravesh is the ceremony of entering a new house for the first time. The aim is to ask for blessings and a peaceful home.. Items needed and the steps, in plain words.

Who is worshipped and when

The puja is for Ganesha, Vastu Purusha and the household gods. It is done: On entering a new home.

Items you need (samagri)

Tick the items as you collect them. The first few are specific to this puja; the rest are the basic items for any puja. You can print this page as a checklist.

Steps

  1. Choose the date and time with your priest from the panchang.
  2. Enter the house with the right foot, with a kalash or lamp carried by the lady of the house.
  3. Worship Ganesha, the kalash and the household deity.
  4. A short havan is done in many families, with the priest chanting.
  5. Boil milk in the new home until it overflows, as a sign of abundance.
  6. Offer prasad, and feed relatives and neighbours.

Notes

Customs differ widely between regions and communities: Tamil, Bengali, Gujarati and Punjabi families each have their own steps. A priest from your own community is the best guide.

Questions people ask

When is Griha Pravesh Puja (House-Warming) done?

On entering a new home. Check a panchang or ask a priest for the exact time in the current year.

Can it be done at home?

Yes. This outline is for a simple home puja. For a long ceremony, a priest is usually asked to lead it.

This page describes common practice in plain words. Families, regions and priests differ in the details, and your own family tradition or your priest comes first. Dates and muhurat (auspicious times) change every year: ask a priest or use a trusted panchang. Nothing here promises any result.
