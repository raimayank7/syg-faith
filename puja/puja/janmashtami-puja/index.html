Janmashtami Puja (जन्माष्टमी पूजा)

Janmashtami celebrates the birth of Krishna. Homes set up a cradle for the infant Krishna (Laddu Gopal) and wait for midnight.. Items needed and the steps, in plain words.

Who is worshipped and when

The puja is for Krishna. It is done: The birth of Krishna at midnight on Janmashtami.

Items you need (samagri)

Tick the items as you collect them. The first few are specific to this puja; the rest are the basic items for any puja. You can print this page as a checklist.

Steps

  1. Fast during the day if you wish.
  2. In the evening, decorate the cradle and the room.
  3. At midnight, bathe the image with panchamrit and water, dress it and place it in the cradle.
  4. Offer butter, mishri and tulsi, and sing the aarti and bhajans.
  5. Break the fast with the prasad. Rock the cradle and distribute prasad.

Notes

In Maharashtra the day is also celebrated with the dahi handi, in which teams form a human pyramid to break a pot of curd.

Questions people ask

When is Janmashtami Puja done?

The birth of Krishna at midnight on Janmashtami. Check a panchang or ask a priest for the exact time in the current year.

Can it be done at home?

Yes. This outline is for a simple home puja. For a long ceremony, a priest is usually asked to lead it.

This page describes common practice in plain words. Families, regions and priests differ in the details, and your own family tradition or your priest comes first. Dates and muhurat (auspicious times) change every year: ask a priest or use a trusted panchang. Nothing here promises any result.
