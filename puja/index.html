Questions people ask

What is puja?

Puja is the Hindu act of worship in which the deity is honoured with offerings of flowers, light, incense and food, with prayers. It can be a full ceremony with a priest or a short daily prayer at home.

Do I need a priest?

Not for a simple home puja. For long ceremonies such as griha pravesh or Satyanarayan katha, families usually ask a priest.

Where do I find the right muhurat?

Ask a priest or use a trusted panchang for your city. Dates differ every year.
