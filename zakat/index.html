Questions people ask

What percentage is Zakat?

2.5% of the net qualifying wealth above the nisab, once a year.

Is the nisab in gold or silver?

Either is used; many zakat organisations use the silver nisab (612.36 g), others the gold (87.48 g). Ask your scholar.

This is a calculator and a plain explanation, not a fatwa. Schools of thought differ on what is zakatable (for example, jewellery, shares and business assets) and on how to value them. For your own case, ask a trusted scholar or a zakat office.
