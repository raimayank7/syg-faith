Mosque timetable maker

Make a monthly prayer timetable with iqamah times for your mosque, then print it or save it as a PDF.

About the timetable maker

Mosques usually put a printed timetable on the notice board each month, showing the time each prayer begins and the time the congregation prayer (iqamah) is held. This tool makes that sheet for you: choose your city, type the name of your mosque, pick the month and the calculation method that your community uses, and set how many minutes after the start of each prayer the iqamah is held. The page then shows a table you can print or save as a PDF.

The prayer times are calculated from the position of the sun at your city, using one of the usual methods, and may differ by a minute or two from the times your own community announces. Many mosques add a few minutes before Fajr and after sunset to be careful. Check the printed table against your local observation or your imam before you pin it up. The guides below explain how to design a timetable that is easy to read from a distance and how adhan and iqamah differ.

Questions people ask

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-in and nothing is stored.

Can I use it for a month in the future?

Yes, for any month. The times are calculated, not looked up.

The times are calculated and the iqamah times are only the delays you enter. Each mosque decides its own times, and many add a margin of caution; please check the sheet against your usual practice and a local scholar before putting it on the notice board. This is not a ruling.
