The prayer of Ibrahim and Ismail

A prayer from the Quran: 2:127-128. The Arabic, the transliteration, the English meaning and a note on the context.

Al-Baqarah 2:127

وَإِذْ يَرْفَعُ إِبْرَٰهِۦمُ ٱلْقَوَاعِدَ مِنَ ٱلْبَيْتِ وَإِسْمَٰعِيلُ رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّآ ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلْعَلِيمُ

Waith yarfaAAu ibraheemu alqawaAAida mina albayti waismaAAeelu rabbana taqabbal minna innaka anta alssameeAAu alAAaleemu

And when Abraham and Ishmael were raising the foundations of the House, (Abraham prayed): Our Lord! Accept from us (this duty). Lo! Thou, only Thou, art the Hearer, the Knower.

Al-Baqarah 2:128

رَبَّنَا وَٱجْعَلْنَا مُسْلِمَيْنِ لَكَ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِنَآ أُمَّةًۭ مُّسْلِمَةًۭ لَّكَ وَأَرِنَا مَنَاسِكَنَا وَتُبْ عَلَيْنَآ ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلتَّوَّابُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Rabbana waijAAalna muslimayni laka wamin thurriyyatina ommatan muslimatan laka waarina manasikana watub AAalayna innaka anta alttawwabu alrraheemu

Our Lord! And make us submissive unto Thee and of our seed a nation submissive unto Thee, and show us our ways of worship, and relent toward us. Lo! Thou, only Thou, art the Relenting, the Merciful.

Context

The verse describes Abraham (Ibrahim) and Ishmael (Ismail) raising the foundations of the House, the Kaaba, and praying as they worked.

Explanation

The prayer is spoken at the moment of doing a good work: first that the work be accepted, then that they and their descendants be submissive to Allah, shown the ways of worship and forgiven. It is a model of asking for acceptance of effort, not only for success. Anyone who builds something good, a home, a school, a family, can borrow its words. The last lines name two of the beautiful names of Allah: the Relenting (At-Tawwab) and the Merciful (Ar-Rahim).

How to use this page

Read the Arabic if you can, and use the letters beneath it to follow the sounds. Questions people ask

Where is this prayer in the Quran?

It is in Al-Baqarah 2:127-128.

Do I have to say it in Arabic?

Many Muslims recite the Arabic of the Quran as it is revealed and also pray in their own language. A dua can be made in any language, in your own words, at any time.
