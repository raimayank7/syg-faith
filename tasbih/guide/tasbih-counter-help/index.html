Using the digital tasbih

How to use the online tasbih counter: tapping, the space bar, goals, vibration on phones, saving counts, installing the page on a home screen and what to do if the count disappears.

Tap to count. Tap the big button, or press the space bar or Enter on a computer. The count goes up by one and the bar shows your progress to the goal.

Goal. The goal is set by the phrase you choose (33, 100 or 108), and you can change it in the Goal box.

Vibrate. On most Android phones the phone vibrates a little on each tap and twice when you reach the goal. iPhones do not allow web pages to vibrate.

Saving. Counts are saved in your browser. If you use a private window, or clear site data, they are lost. The total is only for this device.

Undo and reset. If you tap by mistake, press Undo. Reset clears the count for the phrase you are on.

On a home screen. In the browser menu of your phone choose "Add to Home screen" for a shortcut to the page.

The counter is a simple aid. It stores its numbers only in this browser on your device, and nothing is sent to us. Dhikr and japa are personal practices; ask a teacher of your tradition about wording and counts.
